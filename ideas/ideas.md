# Ideas

Things waiting to be built. Claim one by building it, then add it to the [list](../README.md) with a PR.

- **DVD player screensaver** - The logo bouncing around the screen, drifting for minutes, and sometimes hitting the corner perfectly.
- **Idle Subway Surfer animation** - The endless runner as pure ambient motion for an idle desk device.
- **Beautiful clock animation** - A clock that earns its screen time as an object you glance at for pleasure.
- **Agent status light** - Stop / go / help-needed, driven by your coding-agent sessions: red while they work, green when idle, a distinct state when one is blocked on you.

## Multiplayer

- **Talkie-walkie** - Push-to-talk between two devices, with pairing so easy it feels like magic; or skip pairing and connect two strangers at random.
- **Pulse** - Send someone a poke that lands as a chime on their device. Wordless presence, Journey-style.
